## Dialect Comparison Summary

| Feature | Northeastern (东北话) | Southwestern (四川话) |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **Retroflex (zh, ch, sh, r)** | Clear, preserved | Often softened to z, c, s |
| **Tone 2 & 3** | Slight flattening | Often merged completely |
| **"What"** | 什么 (shénme) | 啥子 (sházi) |
| **Sentence particles** | 啊, 呢 | 嘞, 嘛, 噻 |
| **Rhythm** | Lively, bouncy | Relaxed, melodic |
| **Region** | Heilongjiang, Jilin, Liaoning | Sichuan, Chongqing, Yunnan |
